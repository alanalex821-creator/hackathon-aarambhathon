The product

Insight for the institution. Protection for every student.

PrivacyLens turns protected student data into institutional insight, built on four ideas.

Aggregated insights

Group-level KPIs and trends, such as engagement and attendance.

Role-based views

Each role sees only the dashboard scope it is permitted.

Access controls

Role rules decide who can open detailed rows.

Privacy-first defaults

Small-group suppression and masking, with access events logged.

The workflow

How PrivacyLens works

Four steps, from approved data to insight that protects every student.

STEP 01

Authorized data sources

Approved, de-identified student-course mapping, LMS and attendance exports.

ExampleLMS activity summary · attendance export · assessment summary
STEP 02

Data aggregation

KPIs are calculated at group level, only when the denominator and period are valid.

ExampleAttendance rate = present ÷ scheduled sessions × 100
STEP 03

Role-based access

Role is identified at sign-in; only the permitted scope appears.

ExampleFaculty see assigned courses; analysts use approved extracts
STEP 04

Privacy protection

Small groups are suppressed and identifiers are masked in the default view.

ExampleEvery access event is recorded in an audit log
Why it is different

Careless analytics versus PrivacyLens.

Careless analytics

  • WHO SEES DATAAnyone with dashboard access
  • WHAT IS SHOWNIndividual student records

PrivacyLens

  • WHO SEES DATAOnly the scope each role is permitted

Questions judges ask

Straight answers.

Does PrivacyLens show individual student records?

No. The default view shows group-level KPIs and trends only. Detailed rows are restricted by role, and access events are logged.

What data does it use?

Approved, de-identified student-course mapping, plus LMS and attendance exports. The prototype runs on synthetic demo data (100–500 rows).

Are the numbers on this site real?

No. Every value is illustrative and marked that way. Final formulas, units, owners and source tables are to be agreed with the university.

What happens to small groups?

Groups below a university-approved minimum size are suppressed or combined, and identifiers are masked in the default view.

What is finished, and what is planned?

Working today: role-based sign-in, KPI review and an audit trail. Planned: more authorized data sources, more role-based views, stronger privacy controls from user feedback, and testing on larger datasets.
